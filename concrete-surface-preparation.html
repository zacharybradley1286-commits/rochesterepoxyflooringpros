Professional Diamond Grinding & Slab Restoration

Over 90% of floor coating failures stem from inadequate surface preparation. We never use cheap muriatic acid etching, which introduces gallons of destructive moisture into your slab. Instead, we utilize heavy 3-phase planetary diamond grinders with HEPA dust extractors to open the concrete pores, level high spots, and mechanically remove contaminants before coating application.
