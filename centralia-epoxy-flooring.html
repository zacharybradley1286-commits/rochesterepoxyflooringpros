Frequently Asked Questions in Centralia

Why is concrete moisture testing essential in Centralia?

Because Centralia slabs are subject to high winter water tables and Pacific Northwest maritime moisture. Without measuring moisture vapor emissions and diamond grinding the slab, standard coatings can delaminate from vapor pressure.

How quickly can a garage floor in Centralia be completed?

Most two-car garage epoxy and polyaspartic systems in Centralia are completed in just 1 to 2 days, with light foot traffic permitted in 6 hours and full vehicle parking in 24 to 48 hours.

Do you offer slip-resistant options for Centralia garages and shops?

Yes. We broadcast decorative vinyl flakes and specialized aggregate textures into the base coat to ensure safe, slip-resistant footing even when tracking in wet Washington rainwater.
