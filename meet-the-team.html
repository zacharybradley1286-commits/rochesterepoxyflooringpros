Our Team & Installation Standards

At Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros, our crew specializes exclusively in professional concrete surface preparation, moisture vapor mitigation, and industrial resinous coating systems for residential, agricultural, and commercial properties.

How We Deliver Long-Lasting Floors

When you choose us for your garage, workshop, or commercial facility, you work directly with our dedicated estimators and trained field technicians. We handle your project from initial moisture testing through final clear coat application with strict attention to technical details.

Our Quality Standards on Every Project

  • Certified Equipment & Training: We operate heavy planetary diamond grinders with dual-motor HEPA vacuum filtration to ensure optimal surface profiling without dusting your home.
  • Mandatory Moisture Testing: Slabs in the Chehalis Basin and Thurston County are prone to high hydrostatic vapor pressure. We test sub-slab moisture on every floor before selecting primer chemistry.
  • 100% Solids Formulations: We never apply cheap water-thinned paints. We use high-build, 100% solids industrial epoxy basecoats and aliphatic polyaspartic wear layers.
  • Written Adhesion Warranties: Every installation includes a clear, written warranty covering peeling, hot-tire pickup, and delamination.
  • Transparent Communication: You receive an itemized proposal with clear timelines, preparation specifications, and cure schedules before any work starts.

Why Preparation Trumps Shortcut Solutions

Epoxy floor failures in the Pacific Northwest are never a mystery. They happen when contractors rush the job, acid-etch instead of diamond-grinding, or apply coatings over damp concrete. We take the time to grind to CSP-2 or CSP-3 standards, chase and repair every crack with structural polyurea, and install multi-layer systems engineered for decades of service.
