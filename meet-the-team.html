How Your Project Is Staffed

Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros operates as a local marketing and intake service. Inquiries are matched to an independent concrete coating contractor licensed and insured in Washington State.

What that means for you

When you submit a quote request or call, your project details are routed to the contractor covering your area. That contractor performs the estimate and the installation, holds the license and insurance for the work, and issues the warranty. You are told who they are before any work is scheduled.

What to ask before you hire anyone

This applies to any flooring contractor you consider, not just one reached through this site:

  • Contractor registration number. Washington contractors must register with L&I. Verify it at the Washington State L&I contractor lookup before work begins.
  • Certificate of insurance. Ask for general liability coverage and confirm it is current.
  • Written scope. The estimate should specify the grinding method, the moisture test result, the resin system and total mil thickness — not just a square-foot price.
  • Warranty terms in writing. Ask specifically what voids it and who honors it.
  • Moisture testing. Ask whether it is included before primer. On slabs in this region it should never be optional.

Why the specification matters more than the crew size

Epoxy floor failures in the South Sound are rarely a workmanship mystery. They trace back to a slab that was acid etched instead of diamond ground, or coated without anyone measuring moisture vapor emission first. A contractor who does those two steps properly will outperform a larger outfit that skips them, which is why the questions above are worth more than any claim about years in business.
