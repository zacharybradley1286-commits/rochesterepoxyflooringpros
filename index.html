Durable, Moisture-Mitigating Epoxy Flooring in Rochester

Rochester sits within the Chehalis River drainage basin where high water tables, damp maritime winters, and glacial outwash gravels create persistent hydrostatic vapor pressure beneath concrete slabs. Unsealed garage floors, pole barns, and commercial shops along Highway 12 and Albany Street frequently suffer from dampness, efflorescence dusting, and winter spalling. We solve this by mechanically diamond grinding concrete, applying specialized epoxy moisture barriers, and sealing floors with 100% UV-stable polyaspartics.

Questions We Hear

Why do DIY epoxy kits peel and fail in Washington garages?

Water-based DIY box kits lack mechanical adhesion and moisture mitigation. Acid etching fails to open concrete pores properly, and ground moisture pushes the thin paint off the slab. We eliminate peeling by mechanically diamond grinding the slab to CSP-2/CSP-3 and applying deep-penetrating moisture-barrier epoxy primers.

How long does a professional epoxy floor last?

A professionally diamond-ground, 100% solids epoxy floor with a polyaspartic topcoat typically lasts 15 to 25+ years in residential garages and heavy workshops. We back our installations with a written warranty against peeling and hot-tire pickup.

Can epoxy be installed over cracked or pitted concrete?

Yes. Our surface preparation process includes routing cracks with diamond v-blades and patching pits with rapid-curing polyurea menders sanded flush before primer application.
