Managing Concrete Moisture Vapor Emission Rates (MVER) in the Chehalis Basin

By Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros Editorial Team

Technical breakdown of ASTM F1869 and F2170 moisture testing standards for concrete slabs in high-water-table areas across Southwest Washington.

Quick Answer · Direct Summary

Concrete slabs in high-water-table regions like the Chehalis River basin frequently exceed moisture emission rates of 5–10 lbs/1,000 sq ft/24 hrs, requiring specialized ASTM-compliant moisture vapor barrier primers before epoxy installation.

Next Question (What happens if a coating is installed over high MVER concrete without a primer?): Hydrostatic pressure drives moisture vapor upward, causing osmotic pressure cells to form beneath the coating, leading to large water-filled blisters and coating delamination.

The geography of Rochester, Grand Mound, and South Thurston County is defined by the Chehalis River basin—a region of shallow water tables, heavy winter rainfall, and highly permeable glacial outwash gravels. For concrete slabs poured without thick sub-slab poly vapor barriers, moisture vapor movement through the floor is a constant physical reality.

Moisture Vapor Emission Rate (MVER) measures the volume of water vapor escaping through 1,000 square feet of concrete over a 24-hour window. Standard retail garage paints can only tolerate 3 pounds of vapor drive. Slabs throughout our region routinely test between 6 and 15 pounds of MVER, particularly during the wet months between October and April.

When moisture vapor encounters an impermeable resinous coating, it condenses back into liquid water. If high alkalinity and dissolved concrete salts are present, osmotic pressure cells develop. These osmotic cells exert hundreds of pounds of hydrostatic pressure per square inch, easily blowing bubbles through unreinforced coatings.

Professional concrete contractors evaluate slabs using standardized testing protocols, including ASTM F1869 anhydrous calcium chloride domes and ASTM F2170 in-situ relative humidity probes drilled into the core of the concrete.

When high moisture levels are detected, Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros installs specialized moisture mitigation epoxy primers. Formulated with high-density polyamines, these basecoats withstand continuous moisture emissions up to 20 lbs MVER and 99% internal relative humidity, forming a permanent subterranean vapor lock that guarantees floor longevity.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I tell if my garage slab has high moisture vapor drive?

Tape a 2x2 foot square of clear plastic sheeting to your bare concrete with duct tape. If condensation droplets or dark damp concrete appear beneath the plastic after 24 hours, significant moisture is present.

Do newly poured concrete slabs need moisture testing?

Yes. New concrete requires at least 28 to 45 days to hydrate and dry. Testing ensures residual water from the mix has dropped to safe levels before applying resin.
