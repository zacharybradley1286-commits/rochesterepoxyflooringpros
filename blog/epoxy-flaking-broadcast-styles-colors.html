Full Broadcast Vinyl Color Flake Systems: Design & Traction Guide

By Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros Editorial Team

Explore vinyl color flake sizes, custom color blends, and traction textures to design the perfect custom garage floor in Rochester, WA.

Quick Answer · Direct Summary

Full broadcast color flake systems saturate the wet epoxy basecoat to 100% rejection, creating a seamless 35-mil stone-granite look that hides concrete imperfections and provides built-in slip resistance.

Next Question (What is the difference between partial and full flake broadcast?): Partial broadcast scatters only a few chips over a painted floor, leaving thin spots that easily wear through. Full broadcast blankets the floor completely with over 1 lb of flakes per 10 sq ft, doubling coating thickness.

When upgrading a residential garage or workshop in Rochester, aesthetic design and functional performance must go hand-in-hand. Vinyl color flake systems (also known as decorative color chips) are the industry standard for creating an elegant, multi-tonal granite appearance that dramatically transforms dull gray concrete.

Color flakes are formulated from custom acrylic and vinyl resins that are color-fast, UV-stable, and chemically inert. They are available in standard 1/4-inch chips, micro 1/16-inch blends for an architectural terrazzo look, or bold 1/2-inch chips for a modern stone aesthetic. Popular palettes in the Pacific Northwest include cool slate grays, warm granite earths, and deep Pacific blues.

The critical distinction in quality is the broadcast density. Low-cost contractors cut corners by doing a 'light' or 'medium' broadcast, sprinkling sparse chips across a thin basecoat. This leaves raw basecoat exposed to UV rays, tire abrasion, and visible roller marks.

Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros performs strictly 100% 'full broadcast to rejection' installations. We cast an abundant blanket of vinyl flakes across the wet moisture-barrier basecoat until the entire slab is saturated and cannot hold any more chips—typically using 1 to 1.25 pounds of flakes per 10 square feet.

Once cured, our technicians scrape the excess flakes in multiple directions to achieve an even, comfortable profile before vacuuming and applying dual coats of crystal-clear polyaspartic. The embedded flake matrix creates a resilient 30-to-35 mil shield that hides concrete imperfections, masks everyday dust, and provides subtle, built-in slip resistance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do colored vinyl flakes fade or yellow over time from sunlight?

No. When locked under our UV-stable aliphatic polyaspartic topcoat, the vinyl flakes are 100% protected against UV ambering, sun bleaching, and discoloration.

Can I mix custom flake colors to match my vehicle or workshop branding?

Absolutely. We offer dozens of pre-formulated blends as well as custom multi-color blends tailored to match your cabinetry, cars, or personal design aesthetic.
