Can You Epoxy an Outdoor Patio in the Pacific Northwest? (Polyaspartic vs Epoxy)

By Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros Editorial Team

Why epoxy fails outdoors in Washington and why 100% UV-stable aliphatic polyaspartic coatings are the only permanent solution for exterior patios and walkways.

Quick Answer · Direct Summary

Standard epoxy cannot be used outdoors because UV sunlight causes rapid yellowing, chalking, and brittleness. Exterior patios in Washington require 100% UV-stable aliphatic polyaspartic coatings.

Next Question (What happens to outdoor concrete coated in standard epoxy?): Within 6 to 12 months, outdoor UV radiation breaks chemical bonds in standard aromatic epoxy, turning the clear finish amber yellow and flaking the resin into fine white chalk.

Homeowners in Rochester, Grand Mound, and South Sound love outdoor living, from summer barbecues on covered back patios to relaxing on custom concrete pool decks. However, exterior concrete slabs in Western Washington endure continuous moisture, winter moss and algae growth, and freeze-thaw cycles that leave patios slick, discolored, and pitted.

When homeowners consider coating an exterior concrete patio, porch, or sidewalk, their first thought is often 'garage epoxy.' But installing standard epoxy outdoors is a recipe for rapid aesthetic and structural failure. Epoxy resin contains aromatic chemical rings that are vulnerable to ultraviolet (UV) sunlight.

Under direct or even overcast Pacific Northwest solar exposure, standard epoxy undergoes photochemical degradation. Within months, the clear resin yellows noticeably (ambering), loses its gloss, and begins chalking into a powdery white dust that lifts with foot traffic. Furthermore, rigid epoxies cannot breathe or flex with outdoor seasonal concrete thermal movement.

The engineered solution for outdoor concrete is pure aliphatic polyaspartic technology. Polyaspartic coatings are 100% UV-stable and completely immune to solar yellowing, gloss loss, or chalking, maintaining their crystal-clear depth and vibrant color flake aesthetics indefinitely.

To conquer Pacific Northwest rain and morning dew, we broadcast specialized non-skid aggregates directly into the exterior polyaspartic system. This creates a comfortable, barefoot-friendly texture that guarantees dependable slip resistance around hot tubs, outdoor kitchens, and rainy backyard steps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will outdoor polyaspartic patio coatings prevent green moss and algae growth?

Yes. Because polyaspartic creates a non-porous monolithic surface, moss and algae spores cannot take root in the concrete pores and easily wash away with a garden hose.

Can you coat vertical patio steps and concrete porch risers?

Yes. We coat vertical concrete steps, patio borders, and stem walls using thixotropic vertical bonding agents to create a seamless, cohesive outdoor living space.
