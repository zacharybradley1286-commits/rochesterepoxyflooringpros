How to Clean and Maintain Your Epoxy Garage Floor in Rochester

By Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros Editorial Team

Simple cleaning routines, safe soaps, and stain removal tips to keep your Rochester epoxy garage floor looking brand new for decades.

Quick Answer · Direct Summary

Clean epoxy floors using a soft dust mop for daily care and warm water mixed with a mild, pH-neutral soap or ammonia for occasional deep cleaning.

Next Question (What cleaning products should never be used on epoxy?): Never use harsh citrus cleaners, vinegar, or bleach solutions, as high acidity will slowly etch and dull the high-gloss clear protective finish over time.

One of the greatest advantages of investing in a professional epoxy and polyaspartic floor coating is the remarkable ease of ongoing maintenance. Unlike porous bare concrete that absorbs oil stains and sheds chalky white dust constantly, a seamless resinous floor creates a non-porous shield that repels dirt, fluids, and automotive chemicals.

For routine weekly cleaning, a simple soft microfiber dust mop or push broom is all that is required to sweep out dried dirt, leaves, and lawn clippings tracked in from Rochester yards. Because the coating is completely non-static and non-porous, fine dust sweeps cleanly off the surface without clinging.

🎯 Side Quest: Metallic Epoxy Floors in Rochester: Custom Marble Look for Modern Spaces

When wet winter slush, road salts, or muddy water accumulate beneath parked vehicles, a quick washdown restores showroom shine. Simply mix four to six ounces of clear ammonia or a pH-neutral commercial floor cleaner into a bucket of warm water. Apply with a microfiber mop, rinse with clean water, and pull excess liquid toward the garage apron using a flexible foam squeegee.

If motor oil, transmission fluid, or battery acid drips onto the floor, there is no need to panic. Wipe up the puddle with a paper towel or shop rag, then clean the residue with a squirt of dish soap and warm water. Because 100% solids epoxy and polyaspartic coats are chemically inert, automotive fluids will not soften, stain, or etch the finish if wiped up in a reasonable timeframe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pressure wash my epoxy garage floor?

Yes, you can safely pressure wash the floor using a wide fan tip (40 degrees) at moderate pressure (under 2,000 PSI), keeping the nozzle at least 12 inches away from the surface.

Will road salt damage my polyaspartic topcoat?

No, polyaspartic clear coats are completely impervious to deicing salts and magnesium chloride used on Western Washington mountain passes and highways.
