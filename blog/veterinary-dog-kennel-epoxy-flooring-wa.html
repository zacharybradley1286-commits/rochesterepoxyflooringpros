Sanitary Seamless Epoxy Floors for Kennels & Vet Clinics in Lewis County

By Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros Editorial Team

Antimicrobial, chemical-resistant resinous flooring engineered for dog boarding kennels, veterinary clinics, and animal shelters in rural Southwest Washington.

Quick Answer · Direct Summary

Animal care facilities require non-porous, chemical-resistant epoxy or polyurea floors that resist animal urine uric acid, stand up to harsh disinfectant washdowns, and provide comfortable paw traction.

Next Question (How do you protect walls from animal waste?): We install a seamless 6-inch integral cove base that wraps the floor coating directly up the drywall or concrete block, eliminating seams where urine and bacteria can penetrate.

Operating an animal boarding kennel, dog daycare, or veterinary hospital in Rochester, Centralia, or Chehalis presents unique flooring challenges. Floors must withstand constant heavy foot traffic, sharp animal claws, aggressive urine and fecal acids, and twice-daily washdowns with potent disinfectant sanitizers.

Bare concrete or tile with grout joints is entirely unsuitable for animal care facilities. Concrete quickly absorbs animal urine, allowing bacteria and pungent ammonia odors to penetrate deep into the slab where no amount of surface mop cleaning can reach. Grout lines in tile floors chip and harbor parvovirus and fungal spores.

Our seamless veterinary and kennel floor coatings form an impermeable monolithic barrier that completely isolates concrete from liquids. Formulated with 100% solids industrial epoxy and antimicrobial additives, the surface actively prevents the proliferation of bacteria, ringworm, and viral pathogens.

Chemical resistance is paramount. Veterinary surgical suites and boarding kennels frequently clean with quaternary ammonium solutions, bleach, and enzymatic cleaners. Our high-performance polyaspartic topcoats resist staining, softening, and discoloration from both concentrated disinfectants and concentrated urine acids.

We carefully calibrate the floor texture for animal ergonomics. While smooth floors become treacherous ice rinks when wet, overly rough aggregate can abrade animal paw pads. We engineer an orange-peel fine-flake broadcast that provides secure slip resistance for veterinary technicians while remaining gentle on dogs' paws.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will dog toenails scratch or gouge the polyaspartic finish?

No. Commercial polyaspartic topcoats achieve a Shore D hardness rating of 80 to 85, making them virtually impervious to scratching or dulling from canine claws.

How does the floor prevent lingering animal odors?

Because the floor is 100% non-porous and seamless, animal waste cannot penetrate the surface. Odors wash away immediately with standard sanitizing rinse-downs.
