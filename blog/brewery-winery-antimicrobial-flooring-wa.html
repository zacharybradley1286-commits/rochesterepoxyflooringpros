Antimicrobial Resinous Flooring for South Sound Breweries & Cideries

By Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros Editorial Team

Thermal shock-resistant urethane slurry and anti-slip floors engineered for craft breweries, cider mills, and tasting rooms in Western Washington.

Quick Answer · Direct Summary

Craft breweries and cideries require urethane cement slurry floors with thermal shock resistance up to 250°F and chemical resistance against beer stone, caustic cleansers, and organic fruit acids.

Next Question (Why does beer stone and acidic washdown destroy standard concrete?): Beer, wort, and fruit juices contain organic acids (pH 3–4) that chemically dissolve the calcium silicate matrix of bare concrete, creating pitted ruts and breeding grounds for wild yeasts.

The craft beverage movement in Thurston and Lewis Counties—including breweries, cideries, and meaderies from Olympia to Chehalis—places immense chemical and thermal demands on production floor surfaces. In brewing brewhouses and cellaring areas, floors must handle boiling wort discharges, caustic CIP chemicals, and heavy stainless steel fermenter point-loads.

Raw concrete has zero natural resistance to acidic washdowns. Fermenting sugars, fruit juices, and beer residue (with typical pH levels between 3.2 and 4.2) react chemically with cement paste, dissolving aggregate bonds and creating pitted ruts. Furthermore, boiling 200°F brewing kettle runoff followed immediately by cold rinse water causes extreme thermal shock that delaminates ordinary epoxy coatings.

Our brewery flooring systems are engineered with heavy-gauge urethane concrete slurries. Urethane concrete expands and contracts at the exact same physical rate as the underlying concrete substrate, allowing it to withstand boiling liquid spills and steam wand cleanings without lifting, bubbling, or cracking.

Sanitation is critical in craft fermentation. Wild yeast contamination (like Brettanomyces) and bacterial spoilage organisms thrive in microscopic floor crevices. Our monolithic urethane floors feature seamless perimeter cove bases and pre-sloped trench drain tie-ins that eliminate standing wash water and make CIP sanitizing fast and foolproof.

In customer-facing tasting rooms, we combine this industrial performance with stunning decorative aesthetics. High-traffic taprooms feature custom metallic marble epoxy or full-broadcast vinyl flake designs that provide vibrant visual warmth, easy cleanup of spilled pints, and dependable slip resistance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can urethane brewery floors be sloped toward center trench drains?

Yes. We install pre-sloped urethane cement pitch fills to ensure washdown water drains rapidly toward floor drains without pooling under tanks.

How does the floor texture prevent brewers from slipping in rubber boots?

We broadcast coarse aluminum oxide or silica quartz aggregate directly into the slurry, providing aggressive non-skid traction that grips wet rubber boots safely.
