Double Broadcast Quartz Epoxy: Maximum Durability & Slip Resistance

By Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros Editorial Team

Why double-broadcast colored quartz epoxy systems are the ultimate heavy-duty solution for wet shops, firehouses, and commercial washrooms in WA.

Quick Answer · Direct Summary

Double-broadcast quartz epoxy flooring incorporates ceramic-coated silica quartz granules embedded within multiple resin layers, producing a 1/8-inch thick floor with 12,000+ PSI compressive strength and superior wet slip resistance.

Next Question (Where is quartz epoxy typically installed?): Quartz epoxy is the industry standard for fire station truck bays, commercial pool surrounds, veterinary wash bays, and heavy fabrication shops subject to constant moisture and impact.

While standard decorative vinyl chip floors are ideal for residential two-car garages, certain heavy-duty commercial and municipal environments demand extreme structural durability and aggressive slip resistance. For these rigorous applications, double-broadcast color quartz epoxy systems represent the pinnacle of resinous flooring engineering.

Quartz flooring utilizes industrial-grade, ceramic-coated silica granules rather than soft vinyl flakes. Quartz granules are inherently crystalline and achieve a Mohs hardness rating of 7 (harder than structural steel), providing unmatched abrasion resistance against dragging heavy pallets, steel-wheeled carts, and studded equipment tires.

The installation process builds an exceptionally thick, protective floor. After diamond-grinding the substrate, we install a high-build epoxy basecoat and broadcast colored quartz granules to full saturation. Once cured, excess quartz is swept away, and a second clear epoxy receiver coat is applied with another complete broadcast of quartz.

This double-broadcast methodology produces an impressive 1/8-inch (125-mil) thick seamless protective layer. Locked under heavy-duty clear polyaspartic or chemical-resistant urethane topcoats, the floor achieves compressive strength ratings exceeding 12,000 PSI—nearly three times stronger than standard concrete slabs.

The natural crystalline profile of embedded quartz granules delivers exceptional wet traction that will never wear smooth. For fire stations, commercial truck washrooms, and processing facilities in Thurston County, quartz epoxy guarantees dependable worker safety and a 20+ year lifespan under severe daily operational abuse.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does quartz epoxy compare to flake epoxy in price?

Because quartz requires double the resin and two full aggregate broadcasts to achieve a 1/8-inch thickness, it typically costs 20% to 30% more than a standard single-broadcast vinyl chip system.

Is quartz flooring easy to mop and clean despite its texture?

Yes. The clear polyaspartic topcoat encapsulates the quartz peaks, leaving a rounded, cleanable texture that provides positive slip resistance without snagging mops or collecting grime.
