Airplane Hangar Floor Coatings: Resisting Skydrol & Aviation Fuel

By Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros Editorial Team

High-reflectivity, chemical-resistant epoxy and polyurethane floor systems for private hangars and municipal airfields in South Sound.

Quick Answer · Direct Summary

Aviation hangar floors require specialized chemical-resistant polyaspartic or urethane topcoats formulated to withstand Skydrol hydraulic fluid, 100LL aviation fuel, and heavy aircraft point loads.

Next Question (Why is Skydrol hydraulic fluid so destructive to standard floor coatings?): Skydrol is an aggressive phosphate ester hydraulic fluid that acts like an industrial paint stripper, dissolving standard epoxies within minutes of contact.

Private aviators and aircraft maintenance facilities operating around Chehalis-Centralia Airport (CLS) and Olympia Regional Airport (OLM) understand that hangar concrete requires specialized chemical protection far beyond standard residential paint.

Aircraft maintenance inevitably involves exposure to harsh petrochemicals, including 100LL avgas, Jet-A fuel, isopropyl alcohol deicers, and hydraulic fluids. Phosphate-ester hydraulic fluids like Skydrol are notoriously destructive—a single leak will liquefy and blister standard residential epoxies within minutes.

Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros installs multi-component aviation flooring systems engineered specifically to resist prolonged chemical immersion. Our heavy-build primers anchor deeply into diamond-ground concrete, followed by high-solids chemical-resistant urethane (CRU) or specialized polyaspartic topcoats that are 100% inert to Skydrol and aviation fuels.

High light reflectivity is another critical engineering requirement in modern hangars. High-gloss brilliant white or light platinum finishes bounce ceiling LED lighting underneath aircraft fuselages and wings. This eliminates dark shadows, providing technicians with crystal-clear visibility for pre-flight inspections and mechanical work.

These seamless floors easily support heavy multi-ton point-loads from aircraft landing gear, tugs, and hydraulic jacks. Furthermore, any fluid leaks or dropped mechanical cotter pins are immediately visible on the clean, glossy surface, enhancing flight safety and workshop organization.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you paint custom safety walking lines and aircraft taxi lanes on the hangar floor?

Yes. We install high-visibility OSHA yellow and red safety lines, hazard striping, and custom aircraft silhouette parking guides embedded under the clear polyaspartic topcoat.

How does moisture vapor mitigation protect hangar slabs?

Large unheated hangar slabs in Western Washington experience substantial moisture wicking. Our vapor-mitigating basecoats prevent hydrostatic vapor from blistering the high-performance topcoat.
