Careers at Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros

We are always looking for hardworking, quality-focused individuals to join our installation crew and surface preparation team serving Rochester and the South Sound.

Open Positions

We take pride in doing concrete surface preparation and coating installation the right way. If you value craftsmanship, reliability, and precision, we encourage you to apply.

What We Look For

  • Experience with planetary diamond grinders, shot blasters, or concrete prep equipment (or strong willingness to learn).
  • Attention to detail in crack repair, masking, mixing ratios, and clean jobsite maintenance.
  • Reliable transportation and a valid driver's license.
  • Commitment to safety and quality on every residential and commercial project.
  • Positive attitude and ability to work effectively as part of a high-standard installation crew.

To inquire about current crew openings, call us at (360) 284-1350 or email your resume to info@rochesterepoxyflooringpros.com.
