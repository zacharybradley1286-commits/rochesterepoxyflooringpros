For Flooring Contractors

Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros is a local marketing and intake service for epoxy and concrete coating work in Rochester, Grand Mound, Centralia, Chehalis, Tenino and South Thurston County.

Who we work with

Quote requests from this site are routed to an independent contractor covering the area. If you install resinous flooring in the South Sound and want to discuss taking inquiries from this site, get in touch.

What we expect

  • Current Washington State contractor registration and general liability insurance.
  • Mechanical diamond grinding as standard preparation — not acid etching.
  • Moisture vapor testing before primer selection on every slab.
  • Written, itemized estimates that state the prep method, resin system and warranty terms.
  • Prompt response to inquiries — most homeowners contact more than one contractor.
