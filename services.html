Which system fits your floor?

If you haveTypical systemWhy
Residential garage, daily driverFull flake epoxy with polyaspartic topcoatHides dirt and slab imperfection, adds grip, handles hot tires
Warehouse, shop, or forklift trafficHigh-build 100% solids epoxy with urethane wear coatThicker build for point loading and abrasion

Washdown or food-adjacent areaUrethane cement with coved edgesHandles thermal shock; no wall joint to trap moisture
Slab that is cracked, pitted, or previously coatedSurface preparation firstNothing bonds reliably until the substrate is sound

What every system has in common

Regardless of the finish, the sequence underneath it does not change: moisture vapor testing before primer selection, mechanical diamond grinding to a CSP-2/CSP-3 profile, crack and spall repair, then primer, base coat and topcoat. The decorative choice sits on top of that. When a bid comes in far below the others, the savings are almost always found by removing one of those prep steps — which is also why that floor is the one that fails.
