About Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros

Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros is a dedicated local concrete coatings contractor delivering industrial-grade epoxy, polyaspartic systems, and diamond-ground concrete preparation across Rochester, Thurston County, and North Lewis County.

Our Installation Standards

Premature coating failures happen when installers cut corners on preparation, ignore slab moisture, or apply watered-down DIY paints. In the Pacific Northwest, where damp winters and high water tables create persistent sub-slab vapor pressure, concrete coatings require uncompromising preparation and commercial chemistry.

We approach every garage, workshop, and commercial facility with the same proven sequence: precision moisture testing, planetary diamond grinding to an open CSP-2 or CSP-3 profile, structural crack chasing, and 100% solids resin application topped with UV-stable polyaspartic clear coats.

Mechanical Diamond Grinding

We never acid-wash slabs. We mechanically profile every floor using heavy planetary diamond grinders with industrial HEPA dust extractors for maximum chemical bonding.

Commercial-Grade Chemistry

We install 100% solids industrial epoxy basecoats paired with rapid-curing, UV-stable polyaspartics that resist hot tires, chemicals, oil, and winter road salt.

Transparent Itemized Pricing

Every quote breaks down prep, materials, square footage, and warranty terms upfront — with zero hidden surprises or high-pressure sales tactics.

Written Warranty & Accountability

We stand behind our installations with comprehensive written warranties covering adhesion, peeling, and delamination.
