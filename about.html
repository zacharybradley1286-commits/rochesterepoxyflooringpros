About Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros

Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros connects property owners across Rochester and South Thurston County with professional concrete coating work — built around one idea: the floor fails or lasts based on what happens before the first drop of resin is poured.

Our approach

Most epoxy floor failures in this region trace back to the same two shortcuts: acid etching instead of mechanical grinding, and skipping moisture testing on a slab that sits above a high winter water table. A coating applied over an unprofiled, damp slab will release — usually as hot-tire pickup or edge peeling within the first year or two.

The systems specified through this site are built to avoid exactly that: diamond grinding to a CSP-2/CSP-3 profile, moisture vapor testing before primer, and 100% solids epoxy with a polyaspartic topcoat rather than the 30–40% solids water-based product sold in big-box kits. That specification is the whole point — it is what separates a floor that lasts two decades from one that lasts two winters.

Mechanical Excellence

No acid etching. Every slab is mechanically diamond ground to a CSP-2/CSP-3 profile so the primer keys into open concrete pores.

Commercial-Grade Chemistry

100% solids epoxy base coats and aliphatic polyaspartic topcoats — thermoset resins that do not soften under hot radial tires.

Transparent Pricing

Itemized proposals breaking out prep, materials, square footage and warranty terms, so line items can be compared against other bids.

Local Accountability

Written warranty terms are provided in the estimate, covering adhesion and topcoat performance — read them before you sign.
