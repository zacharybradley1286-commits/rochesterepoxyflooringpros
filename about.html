About Epoxy Flooring in Rochester Washington

Rochester Epoxy Flooring Pros is a local Pacific Northwest concrete coating team dedicated to quality craftsmanship, heavy-duty equipment, and honest customer service.

Our approach

Founded by lifelong Washington tradesmen, we recognized that too many local property owners were being misled by cheap DIY paint kits and unvetted contractors who skipped vital concrete prep. We invested in industrial 3-phase diamond grinders and partnered with leading chemical formulators to deliver industrial-grade resinous flooring systems engineered specifically for the moisture dynamics of the Chehalis Basin and South Puget Sound.

Mechanical Excellence

We never take shortcuts with acid etching; every slab is diamond ground to guarantee a permanent bond.

Commercial-Grade Chemistry

We utilize 100% solids epoxies and pure aliphatic polyaspartics that never peel under hot vehicle tires.

Transparent Pricing

Clear, itemized proposals detailing prep, materials, and warranty coverage with zero surprise upcharges.

Local Accountability

Based right here in South Sound, we stand behind every floor we install with long-term guarantees.
